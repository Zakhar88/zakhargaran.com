Fake stories about AI making money on its own

By: Zakhar Garan · 17 November 2026 · First published on LinkedIn

Have you seen those fake stories about AI making money on its own? And lots of people believe them.

I came across a popular Facebook post: an agent supposedly ran a blog in the background for a whole year, it became so popular that a client came to order a sponsored post for $150, the agent negotiated with him itself and even opened a bank account at Monobank on its own. That last part is the funniest: Monobank might want to review its security rules for dealing with agents.

And at the end of this fantastic story, where the client supposedly did pay the $150, the author of course invites you to a webinar where he will tell you how to earn the same way.

What worries me most is that the comments are full of interested people. We are probably in for many more schemes and AI-assisted scam attempts. Be wary of stories that end with an offer to buy something.
