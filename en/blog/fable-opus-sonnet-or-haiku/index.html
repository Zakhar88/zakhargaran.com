Fable, Opus, Sonnet or Haiku? How not to overpay for AI without losing quality

By: Zakhar Garan · 17 November 2026 · First published on LinkedIn

Anthropic has released Claude Fable 5.2, so which model should you pick for which task?

  • Opus 5.5 is recommended as the default for complex code, at medium effort. If the result is weak, raise the effort level first and only then switch models: it is cheaper, and the cache is preserved.
  • Fable 5.2 is for large, fuzzy tasks where a mistake is expensive, such as rewriting the architecture of a whole product. Its tokens cost twice as much as Opus, but it plans on its own and tests as it goes.
  • Sonnet suits well-defined tasks where speed and budget matter most, such as a bug that is easy to reproduce. It costs half as much as Opus.
  • Haiku is for large volumes of routine work: sorting customer requests or writing short summaries.

You can combine models. For example, Fable can lead the migration of a whole project to a new framework version and hand off updates of individual packages to Sonnet subagents.
