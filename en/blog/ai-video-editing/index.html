AI and video editing: how easy it has become

By: Zakhar Garan · 17 November 2026 · First published on LinkedIn

I used to run two YouTube channels, one about films and one about iOS development. To learn video editing I took a 40-hour course plus dozens of hours of lessons on voice processing. And now? Now AI does it all.

I came across a video about Claude + CapCut, and it turns out AI can do everything: you just explain how to cut the video and which effects to add, give it the files, images or music, and it edits the whole thing itself.

Why CapCut is not the best choice

CapCut currently has no MCP, a special “port” for connecting AI, so an agent works with it very inefficiently. One of the best video editors, the free DaVinci Resolve, has not only MCP but also many built-in AI tools. Through MCP the agent works far more economically and can use those built-in tools too, for example for voice processing.

A tip about thumbnails

I would not generate video thumbnails entirely with AI. There are so many of them now, and they look so alike, that in my opinion they put viewers off.
