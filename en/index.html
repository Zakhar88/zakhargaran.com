My app

Home Rate: відгуки про житло

A free iPhone app where residents review their apartment buildings. Find an address on the map and learn whether you can hear the neighbours, how the lift works and where the nearest shelter is, before you rent or buy. You can review your own building too, anonymously if you prefer. The app is in Ukrainian.
