Home Rate

housing reviews

A free iPhone app where residents review their apartment buildings. Find an address on the map and learn about a building from the people who live there, before you rent or buy.

Download on the App Store

What you can find out

  • Whether you can hear the neighbours and how quiet the building is.
  • How the lift works.
  • Where the nearest shelter is.
  • Other impressions residents have of the building and the complex.

How it works

  • reviews by address on a map
  • every review moderated
  • anonymous if you prefer
  • no ads
  • in Ukrainian
  • iPhone

Review your own building too: it helps people who are still choosing where to live.

Who made Home Rate

Home Rate was created by Zakhar Garan (Гаран Захар), a Senior iOS Engineer and iOS Tech Lead from Ivano-Frankivsk, Ukraine, with over 11 years of native iOS development. He built the app on his own, from code to release and promotion, with an AI-assisted workflow.
